<summary>The story behind this project</summary>

Fortnite has shipped thousands of cosmetic skins since 2017, and most players will never see more than a fraction of them in a live match. Locker previews are limited to what you already own. Third-party galleries are slow, ad-heavy, and rarely updated for the newest drops. We wanted a single, focused utility that does one thing well: let you browse, preview, and apply cosmetic appearances on your own machine without touching your Epic account or paying for anything.

It is designed for players who want to see how a skin looks before committing V-Bucks, for content creators who need clean reference shots, and for anyone curious about the current cosmetic catalog. It is not affiliated with Epic Games, and it is not a cheat — it operates entirely on the client side and is meant for personal use in offline or private contexts. ## Overview

fortnite-skin-viewer is a desktop application aimed at players who want a fast, no-cost way to explore and apply Fortnite cosmetic skins on their own system. The project is built for Windows and focuses on simplicity: install it, open it, pick a skin, and see the result. No accounts, no subscriptions, no telemetry.

This README covers what the tool is designed to do, how to get it running, and what to expect. Anything that depends on the current build is left as a placeholder until verified against the latest release.

## What is fortnite-skin-viewer

It is a local skin utility for Fortnite. It is designed to:

- Let you browse the cosmetic catalog from a single desktop window.
- Preview skins, back blings, pickaxes, and wraps before you commit.
- Apply a selected cosmetic appearance to your local client session.
- Stay out of the way — no background services, no persistent processes.

The project is maintained as a free, open repository. It is not a store, not a marketplace, and not a service. You download it, you run it, you close it when you're done.

## Who It's For
